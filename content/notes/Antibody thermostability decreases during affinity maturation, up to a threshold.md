#### Summary
**In the repertoire studied by Shehata et al., the [[thermostability|thermostability]] of [[antibodies|antibodies]] decreased during [[affinity-maturation|affinity maturation]] until a threshold was reached** [@shehata2019]. This threshold was usually reached within a few mutations, and thermostability could be increased by introducing [[Germline|germline]] mutations [@shehata2019]. This is not a monotonic lineage rule: affinity maturation can co-select compensatory stability mutations that maintain thermodynamic stability despite destabilizing affinity mutations [@julian2017].

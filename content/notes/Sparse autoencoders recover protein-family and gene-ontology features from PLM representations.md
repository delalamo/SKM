#### Summary

**[[Sparse autoencoder|Sparse autoencoders]] trained on [[protein-language-models|protein language model]] representations recover interpretable features associated with protein families, gene names, and Gene Ontology terms.** These features can be extracted from both pooled protein representations and residue-level representations [@gujral2025].

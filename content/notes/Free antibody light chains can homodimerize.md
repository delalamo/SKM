#### Summary
**Free [[light-chains|antibody light chains]] can form homodimers through paired $V_L$–$V_L$ and $C_L$–$C_L$ interfaces that cooperatively stabilize the full-length chain** [@rennella2018; @rottenaicher2023]. The $V_L$–$V_L$ interface resembles the $V_H$–$V_L$ interface of an intact [[antibodies|antibody]], and a C-terminal disulfide can covalently link the homodimer [@brumshtein2014; @rottenaicher2023].

**Native dimerization can protect light chains from amyloid formation.** Mcg $V_L$ dimers remained soluble until dissociation into monomers, while isolated $V_L$ domains in another system aggregated at least tenfold faster than full-length light chains [@brumshtein2014; @rennella2018]. In patient-derived FOR005, a $C_L$ V136G mutation weakened dimerization and promoted proteolytic release of the amyloidogenic $V_L$ domain [@rottenaicher2023].

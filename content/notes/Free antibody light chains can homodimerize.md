#### Summary
**Free [[Light chains|antibody light chains]] can form homodimers through paired VL/VL and CL/CL** [@rennella2018; @rottenaicher2023]. The VL/VL interface resembles the VH/VL interface of an intact [[tags/antibodies|antibody]], and a C-terminal disulfide can covalently link the homodimer [@brumshtein2014; @rottenaicher2023].

#### Structural example

[PDB 1JVK](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/1JVK) is a 1.94 Å crystal structure of the amyloidogenic Sea Bence–Jones protein, with two full-length human lambda light chains forming a homodimer [@bourne2002]. This provides a concrete structural example of free-light-chain association; it does not imply that every light-chain sequence forms the same assembly.

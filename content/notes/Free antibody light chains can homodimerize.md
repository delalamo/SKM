#### Summary
**Free [[light-chains|antibody light chains]] can form homodimers through paired VL/VL and CL/CL** [@rennella2018; @rottenaicher2023]. The VL/VL interface resembles the VH/VL interface of an intact [[antibodies|antibody]], and a C-terminal disulfide can covalently link the homodimer [@brumshtein2014; @rottenaicher2023].

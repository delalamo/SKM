**ProteinGym** is a dataset presented formally by Notin et al. [@notin2023] but used for several years previously, that aggregates deep mutational scanning data and ΔΔG data (e.g., from [@tsuboyama2023]) for evaluation of various deep learning models.

#### Related benchmarks

- [[General-purpose language models can rank protein variants without specialist tools|PG-LLM]] evaluates general-purpose LLMs and specialist predictors on matched, sampled variant sets from ProteinGym, with a separate recent-assay panel.

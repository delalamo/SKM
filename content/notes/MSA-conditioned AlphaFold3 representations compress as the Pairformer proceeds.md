#### Summary

**AlphaFold3's internal representation becomes lower-dimensional as the Pairformer processes [[Multiple sequence alignments|MSA]]-conditioned inputs.** The Pairformer compresses diffuse coevolutionary information into a compact pair-space geometry from which structural and biophysical features are increasingly linearly decodable [@feldman2026].

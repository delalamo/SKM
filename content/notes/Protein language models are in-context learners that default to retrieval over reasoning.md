#### Summary

**[[notes/Protein language models|Protein language models]] can behave as in-context learners, but tend to retrieve evolutionary patterns from supplied homologs rather than reason causally about sequence–fitness relationships.** This can distort the relationship between model likelihood and biological fitness [@kantroo2025].

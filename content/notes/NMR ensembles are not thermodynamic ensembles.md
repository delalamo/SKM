#### Summary
 **Conventional deposited [[NMR]] structure bundles are not Boltzmann-weighted [[Protein dynamics|thermodynamic ensembles]]** [@reinknecht2021]. They are sets of independently calculated models consistent with the experimental restraints, and their spread often reflects structural uncertainty rather than conformer populations. Ensemble-averaged NMR restraints can, however, be integrated with simulations to construct dynamics-bearing ensembles [@lindorfflarsen2005].

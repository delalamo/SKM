#### Summary

**Structure-based methods such as [[notes/Inverse folding|inverse folding]] methods outperform sequence-based methods such as [[notes/Protein language models|PLMs]] on the design of high-affinity antibodies** [@shanker2024]. This is irrespective of whether the language model was trained on antibodies (AbLang) or [[notes/Protein language models|generic proteins]] ([[ESM]]-1v).

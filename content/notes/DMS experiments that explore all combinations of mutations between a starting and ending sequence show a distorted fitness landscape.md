#### Summary

**Deep mutational scanning experiments that explore all combinations of mutations between a starting and ending sequence (for example, a [[Germline|germline]] and [[Affinity maturation|affinity-matured]] variant) lead to a distorted fitness landscape that isn't representative of the landscape explored by either variant** [@brooks2026_C].

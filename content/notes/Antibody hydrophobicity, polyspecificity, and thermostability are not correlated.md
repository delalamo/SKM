#### Summary
**The [[antibody-developability|hydrophobicity]], [[antibody-developability|polyreactivity]], and [[thermostability|thermostability]] of [[antibodies|antibodies]] can be weakly coupled within a given dataset.** Shehata et al. found no correlation among these properties [@shehata2019], whereas a larger human-antibody study found that heavy-chain CDR hydrophobicity and positive charge were associated with polyreactivity [@chen2024polyreactivity].

#### Summary
 **[[notes/Diffusion models|Diffusion]] outperforms [[Inversion of protein folding neural networks|hallucination]] when repurposing [[notes/AlphaFold3|AlphaFold3]]-generation methods for [[notes/Protein backbone design|protein backbone design]]** [@ren2025]. This is in contrast with the comparison between BindCraft [@pacesa2025] and RFDiffusion [@watson2023], which showed more equal performance.

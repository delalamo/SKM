#### Summary
**[[notes/inverse-folding|Inverse folding]] models like [[ProteinMPNN]] can zero-shot predict expression changes arising from mutations in [[Complementarity-determining regions|CDRs]] when the ground truth experimental structure is available**[@chun2026]. This suggests that [[CDR sequence changes are enough to destabilize antibodies|destabilization of antibodies via mutations in CDRs]] may be driving poor expression.

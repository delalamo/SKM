#### Summary

The **quanta hypothesis** proposes that neural networks acquire knowledge and capabilities in discrete chunks, or *quanta*, rather than through wholly continuous improvement. If common quanta are learned before rare ones, a power-law distribution of their frequencies can produce familiar neural scaling laws [@michaud2023; @michaud2026].

#### Summary
**Fab [[thermostability|thermostability]] was not correlated with [[antibody-developability|hydrophobicity]]** [@shehata2019]. 

#### Details
In a panel of B cell-derived [[antibodies|mAbs]], apparent melting temperature and HIC retention time had $R^2 = 0.0126$.

#### Summary
**[[tags/antibodies|Antibodies]] often bind the same target region as a cognate ligand, and occasionally reproduce its detailed contacts** [@abanades2026mimic]. A PDB survey found antibody-ligand epitope overlap in about 4,500 of 5,800 structures containing a target with a cognate ligand. Across more than 7,600 antibody-target structures, only 150 reproduced ligand contacts at the residue level, although about 900 had physicochemically similar interactions.

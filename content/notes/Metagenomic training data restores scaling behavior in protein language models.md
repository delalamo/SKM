#### Summary

**Expanding protein-language-model training from curated UniRef sequences to billions of metagenomic sequences restores approximately log-linear scaling of learned structural signal.** ESMC was trained on roughly 2.8 billion sequences, compared with about 50 million for ESM2, and avoided some of the diminishing returns previously observed when scaling PLMs [@candido2026].

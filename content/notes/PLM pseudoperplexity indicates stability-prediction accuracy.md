#### Summary

**[[Sequence perplexity|Pseudoperplexity]] is somewhat predictive of the accuracy of stability predictions made from [[notes/protein-language-models|protein language model]] representations.** Stability probes perform better on sequences assigned higher likelihood by ESMC, making pseudoperplexity a useful indicator of whether the model represents a sequence reliably [@candido2026].

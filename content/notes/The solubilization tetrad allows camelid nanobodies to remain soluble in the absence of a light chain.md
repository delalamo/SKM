#### Summary
**The solubilization tetrad consists of framework-2 residues 42, 49, 50, and 52 in [[Antibody numbering|IMGT numbering]]** and helps [[Nanobodies|camelid VHH nanobodies]] remain soluble in the absence of a [[notes/Light chains|light chain]] [@vincke2009; @rossotti2021]. These correspond to Kabat heavy-chain positions 37, 44, 45, and 47, respectively. In the VHHs studied by Vincke et al., humanizing residues 49 and 50 increased stability but could reduce solubility; humanizing residues 42 and 52 impaired antigen affinity through changes to the CDRH3 conformation [@vincke2009].

**T-cell receptors** (TCRs) are heterodimeric proteins synthesized by [[T cells]] that bind to peptides presented by [[Major histocompatibility complex I|MHC-I]] and [[Major histocompatibility complex II|MHC-II]].

#### Specificity prediction

[[Sequence-only TCR specificity models often struggle to generalize to unseen epitopes|Generalization to unfamiliar epitopes remains a challenge]]. [[Ensemble features can improve specificity prediction over single structures|Features across predicted structural ensembles]] can improve specificity classification and provide one route beyond sequence-only prediction.

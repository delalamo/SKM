**Display methods** are a set of high-throughput methods for protein engineering in which large numbers of recombinant protein variants are synthesized by phages and tested for binding. It's commonly used to design [[tags/antibodies|antibodies]] or protein [[notes/Protein-protein interactions|binders]]. There are numerous variations of this method.

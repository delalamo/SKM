#### Summary
**A small number of high-energy hot spot interactions can dominate the binding energy of a protein-protein interface** [@clackson1995]. In the human growth hormone-receptor interface, alanine scanning identified a compact cluster of residues that contributed most of the binding energy despite a much larger contact surface.

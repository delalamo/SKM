#### Summary

**Off-target binding was detected in 28% of a tested panel of 174 FDA-approved or clinical-stage monoclonal antibodies** [@dai2026offtarget]. The REAP screen profiled recognition of 6,172 human extracellular proteins and domains; a positive antibody had at least one off-target hit.

#### Details

Specific off-target interactions are not identical to generic polyreactivity or “stickiness.” The study associated off-target recognition with antibody-intrinsic properties and epitope mimicry, and demonstrated that engineering tanezumab could remove detected off-target interactions while retaining intended affinity and developability.

The 28% figure describes this panel and screening assay. It is not an estimate of clinical adverse-event frequency, proof that the remaining antibodies have no possible off-targets, or a measured prevalence for de novo binders.

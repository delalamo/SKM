#### Summary
**Selecting repertoire [[Antibodies|antibodies]] for structural mimicry of a cognate ligand can improve epitope-targeted discovery** [@abanades2026mimic]. In one IL-18RA campaign, ranking 20,000 repertoire sequences by ligand-contact mimicry yielded 11 binders among 31 tested candidates, compared with 0 of 11 candidates selected without mimicry residues. Eight mimicry-selected antibodies reached sub-nanomolar affinity without experimental affinity maturation.

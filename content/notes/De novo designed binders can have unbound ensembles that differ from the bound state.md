#### Summary

**De novo designed binders can have unbound ensembles that differ from the bound state.** A successful design need not remain in its intended complex conformation when isolated; conformational reorganization can accompany binding [@britton2026].

#### Details

For a designed RelE binder, replica-exchange simulations sampled an unbound ensemble differing from the predicted complex conformation. Molecular dynamics and hydrogen-deuterium exchange measurements together supported a change on binding. The bound reference was a computational complex model, so this is not a direct high-resolution determination of both ensembles.

The general finding concerns possible [[notes/Protein dynamics|conformational heterogeneity]], not a claim that every designed binder is flexible. Unbound flexibility alone does not distinguish [[Antibody-antigen binding modes are not necessarily defined by induced fit|conformational selection from induced fit]], nor does simulated sampling by itself establish equilibrium populations.

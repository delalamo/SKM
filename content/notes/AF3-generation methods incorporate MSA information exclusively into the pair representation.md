#### Summary
**[[alphafold3|AlphaFold3]]-generation methods like Boltz and Chai exclusively incorporate [[Multiple sequence alignments|MSA]] information into the pair representation, in contrast with [[Evoformer]]-based methods that reason over the information in the folding trunk** [@abramson2024]. Mechanistic probing of AlphaFold3 likewise found that structural signal becomes concentrated and increasingly linearly decodable in the pair representation as the Pairformer proceeds [@feldman2026].

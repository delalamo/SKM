#### Summary
**In [[tags/antibodies|antibodies]] during [[notes/affinity-maturation|affinity maturation]], antigen-binding affinity and broad specificity present competing objectives, with the former achieved by rigidification and the latter by maintained flexibility while targeting conserved epitopes** [@ovchinnikov2018]. A quantitative model captures some degrees of the trade-off between these two.

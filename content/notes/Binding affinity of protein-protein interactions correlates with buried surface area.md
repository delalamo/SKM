#### Summary
**The binding affinity between [[protein-protein-interactions|two proteins]] correlates coarsely with buried surface area** [@chen2013]. At least 500 $Å^{2}$ are required for a stable complex to form [@day2012], and the effect levels off at >2000 $Å^{2}$ [@chen2013]. For a given buried surface area, affinity can span several orders of magnitude, in part because [[A small number of high-energy hot spot interactions can dominate protein-protein binding energy|a small number of hot spot interactions can dominate binding energy]] [@chen2013; @clackson1995].

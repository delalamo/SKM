#### Summary
**Overstabilization can reduce the [[Antibody-antigen binding affinity prediction|affinity]] of [[Nanobodies|nanobodies]]** [@ketaren2023]. In S1-RBD-14, the destabilizing E89K substitution increased affinity from $K_D = 25$ nM to $0.3$ nM, while further stabilization reduced binding. This qualifies [[Antibody affinity can be improved by stabilization]]: the relationship between stability and affinity is non-monotonic.

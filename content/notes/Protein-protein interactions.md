**Protein-protein interactions** describe when two or more proteins bind to one another.

#### Experimental coverage

[[Protein interface clusters rarely span experimental structure determination methods|Interface clusters have limited overlap across experimental methods]], so a collection from one method need not represent the full range of observed binding geometries.

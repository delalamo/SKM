#### Summary

**[[alphafold3|AlphaFold3]] benefits more from phylogenetically diverse [[Multiple sequence alignments|MSAs]] than from deep alignments dominated by near-identical sequences.** A small set of divergent homologs can anchor the model's latent geometry, whereas highly redundant or synthetic junk alignments provide little benefit [@feldman2026].

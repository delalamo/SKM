#### Summary
Changes in the sequence of [[Complementarity-determining regions|CDRs]] is sufficient to destabilize antibodies and lower [[thermostability]] [@wan2026]. A single antibody framework can, with different CDRs, have melting temperatures ranging from 40° to 80° C.

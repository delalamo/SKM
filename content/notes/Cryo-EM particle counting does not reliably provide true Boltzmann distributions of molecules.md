#### Summary

**Counting particles collected with [[Cryo-EM|cryo-EM]] can fail to recover the true [[Boltzmann distribution]] when experimental noise and conformational uncertainty make particle assignments ambiguous** [@evans2025]. At low noise, counting can recover population estimates; bias increases with noise and with decreasing distinguishability between conformations. Methods that infer a distribution from the full particle stack, such as cryo-BIFE, are more robust to these ambiguities [@giraldobarreto2021].

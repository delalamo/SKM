#### Summary
**Nonredundant [[Nanobodies|nanobody]]-antigen structural datasets can still overrepresent a small number of epitopes** [@medved2026]. AVIDbase-nr contains 72 SARS-CoV-2 spike RBD complexes but only eight unique epitope clusters; ricin chain A has 24 complexes and eight epitopes, while hen egg-white lysozyme has nine complexes and three epitopes. AVIDbase also manually corrects biological assemblies, crystal contacts, packing strain, missing density, and post-translational modifications that automated structural-data pipelines can retain.

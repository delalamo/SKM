The **Fab** of an [[tags/antibodies|antibody]] comprises the heavy- and light-chain [[Variable-regions|variable regions]] and their first [[Constant-regions|constant domains]]. Conventional antibodies have two Fabs. [[Nanobodies]] are isolated VHH single variable domains and are not Fabs [@ketaren2023]. Fabs are sometimes used for biophysical studies and have been explored for therapeutic applications.

#### Observations

* **[[tags/antibodies|Antibody]] fragments such as Fabs are cleared from circulation more rapidly than full-length Abs** [@pimm1989].
* **Recombinant Fab and related antibody fragments can be expressed in _E. coli_** [@carter1992].
* **Fabs can enter tumors rapidly, but rapid clearance can limit tumor exposure and retention.** Extending Fab circulation through albumin binding increased tumor accumulation [@dennis2007].

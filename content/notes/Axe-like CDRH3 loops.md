**Axe-like [[Complementarity-determining regions#CDRH3|CDRH3]] loops** are those that are adopt a double-bent conformation ending with a beta sheet, and share features of both [[Kinked CDRH3 loops|kinked]] and unkinked CDRH3 loops. These are found in some [[Broadly neutralizing antibodies|broadly neutralizing antibodies]] and can be predicted with some confidence [@habib2024]. An important sequence motif is D111.2, D111.3, G112.4, S112.3, G112.2 ([[Antibody numbering|IMGT numbering]]), although this was only determined in silico using structure prediction.

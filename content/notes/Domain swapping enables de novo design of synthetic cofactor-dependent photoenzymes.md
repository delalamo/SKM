#### Summary
**A domain-swapped dimer can create a protected inter-domain pocket for synthetic cofactor binding in a *de novo* photoenzyme** [@kim2026eyclamp]. EYClamp binds the photocatalyst Eosin Y with $K_d = 10$ nM and lengthens its triplet excited-state lifetime 29-fold. Fusions to six E3 ligases supported live-cell proximity labeling with aryl-diazirine-, aryl-azide-, and phenol-biotin probes; quantitative proteomics identified 1,633 candidate neighbors for KEAP1, MDM2, ASB7, and STUB1.

#### Summary

**Learned capabilities are better understood as distributed features and circuits than as individual neurons.** Neurons can be polysemantic, while sparse feature directions and interacting subnetworks provide a more natural substrate for the discrete competencies proposed by the [[Quanta hypothesis|quanta hypothesis]] [@michaud2026].

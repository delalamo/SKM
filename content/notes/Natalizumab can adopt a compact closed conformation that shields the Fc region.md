#### Summary

**Solution measurements support a predominantly compact, closed conformation of the IgG4 antibody natalizumab, with the [[Fab]] arms folded toward the Fc region** [@chrone2026]. This limits access to Fc-associated sites and differs from the familiar open Y-shaped depiction of [[tags/antibodies|antibodies]].

#### Details

Cross-linking mass spectrometry, structural modeling, and biophysical and immunochemical assays support the closed arrangement. Diagnostic cross-links were already present after one minute, arguing against closure arising only during prolonged cross-linking.

Increased Concanavalin A binding at 57-67 °C was interpreted as increased accessibility. Low-pH evidence was less decisive: the authors note that altered cross-linking efficiency could explain some apparent changes. Antigen binding as a trigger for opening and effector activation is a proposed mechanism, not directly demonstrated here.

The authors relate this result to earlier polyclonal IgG1-4 and monoclonal IgG1 observations, but explicitly caution that it need not apply to every IgG4. The closed state is an ensemble, not a single rigid structure.

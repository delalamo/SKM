#### Summary

**Structure-conditioned [[inverse-folding|inverse folding]] selects more evolutionarily conserved design positions than sequence-only [[protein-language-models|protein language models]]** [@mcdonald2026]. When selecting positions for site-saturation mutagenesis of [[Rubisco]], ESM-IF1 recommendations were more conserved than those from ESM-1b.

ESM-IF1 evaluated both the unactivated open conformation and the activated closed conformation, with both structures bound to a non-hydrolyzable transition-state analogue.

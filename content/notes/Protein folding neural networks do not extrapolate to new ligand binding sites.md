## Summary

**Protein folding neural networks do not extrapolate to new ligand binding sites** ([@doelsnitz2024], [@drr2024]). [[notes/alphafold2|AlphaFold2]] could not predict a new calcium-binding site in an engineered protein, and instead introduced a fictitious salt bridge [@doelsnitz2024]. Separately, neither [[notes/alphafold3|AlphaFold3]] nor [[notes/rosettafold|RosettaFold-AA]] could predict that a binding site prefers cobalt over copper [@drr2024].

#### Summary
**[[Cytokines]] can be added to antibody [[Complementarity-determining regions|CDRs]] while maintaining correct folding of both components** [@didonato2025]. Here, a monomerized IL-10 cytokine was added to the [[Complementarity-determining regions#CDRL1|CDRL1]] of Palivizumab, although all six were tested.

**B cells** are a type of lymphocyte that synthesizes and secretes antibodies as well as B-cell receptors. Each cell synthesizes a single antibody sequence throughout its life cycle (a phenomenon termed [[Allelic exclusion and inclusion|allelic exclusion]]). Can be categorized as either naive B cells or memory B cells. Activation by antigen presentation causes them to proliferate into effector cells that secrete antibodies.

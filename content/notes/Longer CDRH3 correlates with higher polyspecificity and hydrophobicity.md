#### Summary
**Long [[Complementarity-determining regions#CDRH3|CDRH3]] loops are enriched among [[antibody-developability|polyreactive]] antibodies in some repertoires but are not strongly predictive on their own.** Long, protruding CDRH3 loops characterize some polyreactive antibodies, but many polyreactive antibodies lack this feature [@lecerf2023]. In a nanobody dataset, CDR3 length alone had near-random predictive performance for polyreactivity [@harvey2022].

#### Summary

**Independent [[notes/Immune repertoires|immune repertoires]] can converge on the same [[Germline|V genes]] when responding to a particular antigen.** For example, seven of nine human anti-rabies antibodies described in a review used the VHIII family, while several mouse responses were similarly restricted to one or two VH genes [@rees2020].

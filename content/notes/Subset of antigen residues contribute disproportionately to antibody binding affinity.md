#### Summary
A subset of antigen contacts contribute disproportionately to affinity[@farady2009]. This is frequently observed in cases where [[tags/antibodies|antibodies]] against a human antigen lose substantial affinity for orthologs, for example MT-SP1 (200-300x lower binding to mouse[@farady2009]) and LAMP-1 (complete abrogation despite one residue difference[@pruvost2023])

#### Summary

**A small fraction of [[tags/antibodies|antibodies]] in naive [[Immune repertoires|immune repertoires]] bind previously unseen antigens, with measured frequencies varying by antigen and assay.** Across 15 HIV-seronegative donors, 0.0056% of naive [[B cells]] bound eOD-GT8, an engineered mimic of the HIV-1 Env CD4-binding site, or about one in 18,000 [@jardine2016]. For the SARS-CoV-2 receptor-binding motif, the published study reported median frequencies of 0.0025% among total B cells and 0.0029% among naive B cells, approximately one in 34,000 naive cells [@feldman2021]. These measurements count probe-binding cells, rather than unique antibody sequences.

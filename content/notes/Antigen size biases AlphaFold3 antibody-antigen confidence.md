#### Summary
**[[notes/alphafold3|AlphaFold3]] [[tags/antibodies|antibody]]-antigen confidence is biased by antigen size** [@solanki2026]. Larger antigens lead to broader [[notes/pae|PAE]] distributions, presumably because there are more ways to be very wrong in larger antigens than smaller antigens.

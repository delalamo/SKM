#### Summary
 **Allostery can be mediated by changes in thermal fluctuations without a change in mean structure** [@cooper1984; @tzeng2012]. This is one possible mechanism, in contrast to but not excluding a "domino model" of propagated structural changes as proposed by [@monod1965].

#### Details
Per [@wankowicz2025]:
> "proteins and other biological macromolecules may have evolved to take functional advantage not only of mean conformational states but also of the inevitable thermal fluctuations about the mean."

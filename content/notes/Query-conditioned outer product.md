#### Summary

**Query-conditioned outer product** is an update included in MSA Pairformer that allows it to weight homologous sequences differently[@akiyama2026]. This was shown to improve [[Homooligomeric proteins can undergo many stoichiometric changes in the course of evolution|weakly conserved inter-protomer contacts in homo-oligomers]].

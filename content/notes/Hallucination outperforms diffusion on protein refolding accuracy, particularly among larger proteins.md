#### Summary
 **[[notes/protein-backbone-design|De novo protein backbones]] designed by [[Inversion of protein folding neural networks|hallucination]] are more likely to be recovered by [[notes/structure-prediction|structure prediction neural networks]] than those designed by [[notes/diffusion-models|diffusion]]** (such as RF-diffusion; [@frank2024]). This is particularly true of larger designs.

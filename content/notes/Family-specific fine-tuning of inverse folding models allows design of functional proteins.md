#### Summary

**Fine-tuning [[Inverse folding|inverse folding]] models on a family-specific subset of structural models allows functional proteins to be designed** [@qin2026]. This was demonstrated on nine design tasks that require conserved residues to be correctly placed. It also led to improvements in zero-shot [[Variant effect prediction|variant effect prediction]], shown below.

#### Summary

**Smooth aggregate training curves can hide the discrete acquisition of individual capabilities.** When many small [[Quanta hypothesis|quanta]] are learned at different times and occur with power-law frequencies, their step-like improvements sum to an apparently smooth loss curve [@michaud2023; @michaud2026].

#### Summary

**Only 8% of the PDB interface clusters produced by Foldseek-Interface contain structures determined by more than one experimental method** [@strom2026]. Available interface collections therefore differ substantially across methods.

#### Details

The analysis clustered approximately 3.1 million dimers into 77,167 interface groups. The 8% refers to clusters, not to all protein pairs studied by multiple methods (Figure S2h). It does not establish that changing the experimental method causes a different interface: differences in the proteins selected for each method can also contribute.

As a separate check, only 16% of NMR entries split into multiple interface clusters when the authors clustered models within each entry. The conformational variation captured in those ensembles therefore usually preserved cluster membership.

#### Summary
**Blinded prospective [[tags/antibodies|antibody]]-design methods performed better at [[notes/affinity-maturation|affinity maturation]] when [[Complementarity-determining regions#CDRH3|HCDR3]] was held fixed than at [[Computational ranking of HCDR3 clones can underperform random selection|HCDR3-cluster ranking]] or out-of-library CDR design** [@erasmus2026]. The fixed-HCDR3 affinity-maturation task produced several [[notes/antibody-developability|developable antibodies]] with affinities below 100 pM. These successes were exceptions that did not transfer across tasks: nearly every method underperformed random selection in HCDR3-cluster ranking, and out-of-library CDR design was highly variable.

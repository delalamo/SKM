#### Summary

**Filters identified in retrospective analyses from round-one _de novo_ enzyme design campaigns can be applied in subsequent rounds for lead optimization** [@li2026_B]. This was observed when running partial [[Diffusion models|diffusion]] with AlphaProtein Novo, using geometric active-site filters.

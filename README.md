This repository is the source for [notes.delalamo.xyz](https://notes.delalamo.xyz), a personal knowledge base about machine learning for protein structure, engineering and design, property prediction, and related topics. Citations are collected in `bibliography.bib`; the remaining configuration and source files customize the Quartz site that publishes the notes.

The notes are being rebuilt as an annotated research knowledge base: paper notes with colour-coded margin annotations, paper-agnostic claim notes that gather the evidence for and against each claim from every paper, topic pages, a glossary and concept notes. The new notes live in `content/Papers`, `Claims`, `Topics`, `Concepts`, `Glossary`, `Datasets` and `Indexes`; the earlier zettelkasten notes remain in `content/notes/` (with tag pages in `content/tags/`) until they are migrated. `content/` also opens as an Obsidian vault.

- [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): the rules for editing the knowledge base and how to run its tools (`_tools/`).
- [The tagging guide](docs/tag-taxonomy.md): the topic hierarchy of the legacy notes.
